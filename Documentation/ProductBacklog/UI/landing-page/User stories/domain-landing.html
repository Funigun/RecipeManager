Dishes, recipes, cookbooks, and meal plans

Dishes are what you plan to eat

A dish can have one or more preparation recipes, or be planned on its own.

Example: Tomato & basil pasta can be planned with a recipe or on its own.

Recipes include reusable preparations

Recipes describe how to prepare a dish or an ingredient. A preparation recipe can be reused in other recipes.

Example: Roasted tomato sauce can be used in a pasta recipe and shakshuka.

Keep recipes in cookbooks

Group recipes into cookbooks and nested categories that make sense for your kitchen.

Example: Weeknight favorites → Pasta → Tomato & basil pasta.

Plan the meals you want to make

Schedule dishes by day and meal, and choose the preparation recipe you plan to make when there are alternatives. Generate a shopping list from the plan when you need one.

Example: Thursday dinner · tomato & basil pasta.

Shared units and categories

Choose from shared measurement units and ingredient and recipe categories to keep your cooking organized.
