# Shopping Lists

## Summary
Generate shopping lists on demand from planned Dishes or directly selected Recipes. Use the selected preparation Recipe for a planned Dish when present; otherwise list the Dish under a system-managed category. Expand preparation ingredients, normalize quantities when applicable conversions exist, retain unconverted amounts separately, and explain how totals were calculated.

## Users and permissions
See the shared roles and permissions in the [domain redesign overview](../overview.md#users-and-permissions).

## Domain rules

### Shopping-list generation

- **R-17 — Prepared-ingredient shopping behavior** *(Entities: Ingredient, preparation recipe, shopping list; Source: D-17)*
  - Shopping lists expand ingredients that have preparation recipes into those recipes' ingredients.
  - Ingredients without preparation recipes remain shopping-list items and require a shopping-list category. See [Categories](../categories/prd.md#domain-rules) (R-04).
- **R-18 — Plan-based shopping quantities** *(Entities: Meal-plan entry, Dish, Recipe, shopping list; Source: D-16, D-18)*
  - Each Dish entry in a day represents one serving.
  - For a planned Dish with a selected Recipe, a plan-based shopping list uses that Recipe's ingredients and scales saved quantities to planned servings divided by the Recipe's saved serving count.
- **R-19 — Direct recipe shopping quantities** *(Entities: Recipe, shopping list; Source: D-19)*
  - A shopping list generated by directly selecting a recipe uses its saved amounts without scaling.
- **R-20 — Shopping-list conversion explanation** *(Entities: Ingredient, unit conversion, shopping list; Source: D-20; assumption)*
  - Shopping-list amounts normalize to a global ingredient's primary unit when an applicable conversion is available.
  - The list includes supporting information explaining conversions used; its detailed presentation is deferred.
- **R-30 — Preparation-recipe scaling** *(Entities: Ingredient, preparation recipe, recipe; Source: D-29)*
  - A preparation recipe's saved ingredient amounts scale by the required prepared-ingredient amount divided by the preparation recipe's total output amount.
  - Serving count does not determine this physical output conversion.
- **R-31 — Shopping-list persistence** *(Entities: Shopping list; Source: D-28)*
  - Shopping lists are derived on demand and are not persisted.
- **R-32 — Unconverted shopping-list amounts** *(Entities: Ingredient, unit conversion, shopping-list item; Source: D-32)*
  - If an ingredient-unit conversion is unavailable, the amount remains in its original unit and is marked as unconverted.
  - It is not aggregated with amounts in another unit.
- **R-45 — Planned Dish without a selected Recipe** *(Entities: Meal-plan entry, Dish, shopping list; Source: D-16)*
  - If a planned Dish has no selected Recipe, the shopping list includes the Dish under the dedicated "Dishes" category.
  - Admins cannot manage this category.

Plan-based scaling uses the recipe's saved serving count as described in [Recipes](../recipes/prd.md#domain-rules) (R-27).

## User stories

### US-11 — Generate a shopping list from a meal plan
As a user, I want a shopping list based on planned Dishes so that ingredient quantities reflect the selected preparation and planned servings.

**Acceptance criteria**
- **AC-18:** Given a plan-based list includes a Dish with a selected Recipe, when the list is generated, then the Recipe's saved ingredient amounts are scaled by planned servings divided by its saved serving count.
- **AC-39:** Given a plan-based list includes a Dish with no selected Recipe, when the list is generated, then the Dish appears under the dedicated "Dishes" category, which admins cannot manage.

**Related domain rules:** R-18, R-27 (Recipes), R-45

### US-12 — Generate a shopping list from a directly selected recipe
As a user, I want a shopping list from a directly selected recipe so that it uses the recipe's saved quantities.

**Acceptance criteria**
- **AC-19:** Given a recipe is selected directly, when the list is generated, then its saved amounts are used without scaling.

**Related domain rules:** R-19

### US-13 — Expand prepared ingredients in shopping lists
As a user, I want ingredients with preparation recipes expanded into their component ingredients so that a shopping list reflects what must be prepared.

**Acceptance criteria**
- **AC-20:** Given an ingredient has a preparation recipe, when it is included in a shopping list, then it is expanded into that recipe's ingredients with saved amounts scaled by the required prepared-ingredient amount divided by the preparation recipe's total output amount; an ingredient without a preparation recipe appears as a shopping-list item and requires a shopping-list category.

**Related domain rules:** R-17, R-30, R-04 (Categories)

### US-14 — Normalize shopping-list quantities when conversions are available
As a user, I want shopping-list quantities normalized when possible and explained so that I can understand the resulting totals.

**Acceptance criteria**
- **AC-21:** Given shopping-list amounts for a global ingredient are combined, when conversions are available, then totals are normalized to that ingredient's primary unit and include supporting information explaining the conversions used.
- **AC-27:** Given an ingredient amount has no available conversion to the applicable primary unit, when it appears in a shopping list, then its original amount and unit are retained, it is marked as unconverted, and it is not combined with an amount in another unit.

**Related domain rules:** R-20, R-32

### US-15 — Generate shopping lists on demand
As a user, I want shopping lists generated when requested so that lists reflect the selected plan or recipes without being persisted.

**Acceptance criteria**
- **AC-26:** Given a user generates a shopping list from a plan or directly selected recipes, when generation completes, then the list is derived on demand and is not persisted.

**Related domain rules:** R-31

## Open questions
- The detailed presentation of shopping-list conversion information remains deferred.

## Impact on existing system
Existing generation expands preparation-recipe ingredients and normalizes amounts. The target distinguishes plan-based Dish entries from direct Recipe selection, uses a planned Dish's selected preparation when present, lists a Dish without a selected Recipe under the system-managed "Dishes" category, scales preparation recipes by required output amount, uses available global ingredient conversions, preserves unconverted amounts separately, and provides conversion explanations. Lists are generated on demand and not persisted.
