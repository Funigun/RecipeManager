Lemon & Ricotta Rigatoni with Crispy Capers

schedule Prep: 15m
·
local_fire_department Cook: 18m
·
group Servings:
4

Ingredients

8 items
tips_and_updates

Tip: Reserve at least 1/2 cup of salty starchy pasta water right before draining. It binds the lemon ricotta into a glossy emulsion.

Instructions

4 steps
1

Boil the rigatoni

Bring a large pot of heavily salted water to a rolling boil. Drop the rigatoni and cook until just shy of al dente, about 11 minutes.

2

Crisp the capers

Heat 3 tablespoons of extra-virgin olive oil in a wide skillet over medium heat. Fry the dried capers until blistered and crisp, about 3 minutes. Remove with a slotted spoon and drain on parchment.

3

Fold the ricotta base

In a serving bowl, whisk ricotta, lemon zest, lemon juice, grated Parmigiano, and sliced garlic with 1/4 cup hot pasta cooking water until velvety and loose.

4

Toss and finish

Transfer hot drained pasta directly into the bowl. Toss vigorously to coat every tube. Scatter crispy capers, cracked black pepper, and torn fresh basil over top just before plating.
