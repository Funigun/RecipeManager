## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a clean Scandinavian culinary sensibility—bright, intentional, and tactile. It bridges the precision of a professional chef’s mise en place with the inviting warmth of an airy home kitchen. The aesthetic is anchored in organic freshness, functional clarity, and high-legibility kitchen usability.

### Core Brand Attributes
- **Organized & Chef-Grade:** Structured lists, clear measurements, and distraction-free execution paths that inspire confidence while cooking.
- **Warm Scandinavian Minimalism:** Abundant light, clean negative space, warm oat undertones, and purposeful natural accents over visual clutter.
- **Vibrant & Sensory:** High-resolution food photography paired with botanical greens and sunlit citrus accents to evoke fresh produce, herb gardens, and warm ceramics.

### Design Style & Finish
The style blends modern Scandinavian minimalism with functional tactility. Surfaces evoke matte ceramic stoneware, crisp parchment, and slate countertops. Visual layers rely on soft, natural light casting ultra-subtle ambient drop shadows rather than heavy skeuomorphic bevels. Interactions are snappy, responsive, and legible at arm's length for hands-on kitchen environments.

## Colors

The palette balances deep botanical greens, sunlit spice accents, and neutral ceramic whites to cultivate an organic, kitchen-table environment. 

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Herb Palette (`#2D6A4F`):** Anchors brand identity, primary navigation, major action buttons, active states, and ingredient check marks. Tonal shades include Deep Forest (`#1B4332`) for text contrast and Vibrant Mint/Sage (`#52B788`) for positive tags and active highlights.
- **Secondary Spice Palette (`#E76F51`):** A warm saffron and roasted chili tone reserved for high-energy interactions, active step timers, cooking mode highlights, and book cover badges. Supported by Citrus Zest (`#F4A261`) and Golden Mustard (`#E9C46A`) for dietary badges and ratings.
- **Neutral Stoneware & Surfaces:** 
  - **Light Mode:** Canvas begins on Warm Oat/Porcelain (`#FAF9F6`), transitioning into Crisp Ceramic White (`#FFFFFF`) card containers, outlined by Pale Basil Mist (`#F4F7F5`) and subtle slate dividers (`#E2E8F0`).
  - **Kitchen Dark Mode:** Deep Evergreen Slate (`#0F1715` base, `#162420` surface, `#1E332D` container) engineered specifically for low-light, counter-top readability, avoiding glare on greasy or steamy screens.
- **Typography Neutrals:** Deep Charcoal (`#1A202C`) maintains crisp, readable text for dense ingredient weights, shifting to Slate Gray (`#64748B`) for secondary metadata and timestamps.

## Typography

Typography balances architectural refinement with rapid glancing efficiency. 

- **Display & Headlines (Outfit):** Delivers clean Scandinavian geometric warmth with open counters and balanced curves. Outfit creates clear visual hierarchy on recipe editorial headers, cookbook spines, and large cooking mode steps.
- **Body & Numerical Readouts (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its humanist geometry, tall x-height, and open apertures. Fractions, weights (grams, ounces), and ingredient lines render without typographic collision.
- **Timer & Metrics (`timer-display`):** Tabular figures ensure that seconds, minutes, and temperatures do not shift layout during real-time countdowns.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt dynamic fluid grid with proportional gutters that prioritize scannable ingredient cards, rich photography, and touch-target clearance.

### Grid Systems & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `margin-mobile` (1rem) and `gutter-sm` (1rem). Ingredient rows stretch edge-to-edge. Action bars pin to bottom thumb-reach zones.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `gutter` (1.5rem). Enables split-pane views: ingredient panel pinned to the left, preparation steps scrolling on the right.
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column layout with `margin-desktop` (3rem) and `gutter-lg` (2rem), constrained to a maximum content container of 1320px for optimal reading line length (60–75 characters per instruction line).

### Spacing Philosophy
Dense metadata (diet tags, times, measurements) stays tight using `space-xs` and `space-sm`. Generous breathing room (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) isolates discrete recipe phases and cookbook sections to avoid visual fatigue during active food prep.

## Elevation & Depth

Elevation conveys tangible physical layers reminiscent of stacked cutting boards, recipe index cards, and ceramic plates. Depth is communicated primarily through distinct tonal surfaces and diffused ambient lighting.

### Surface Tiers
1. **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Oat Porcelain (`#FAF9F6`) in light mode; Deep Slate (`#0F1715`) in dark mode.
2. **Level 1 (Cards & Panels):** Pure Ceramic White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px soft slate border (`#E2E8F0`). In dark mode, `#162420` surface with `#1E332D` borders.
3. **Level 2 (Active Steps & Floating Bars):** Raised cards with subtle herbal tinting and dual ambient shadows: `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(45, 106, 79, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
4. **Level 3 (Modals, Timers & Cook Mode HUD):** Layered above background blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with focused drop shadows: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.16)`.

Avoid harsh drop shadows. Lighting must remain soft, diffused, and naturally angled from above.

## Shapes

The shape system employs roundedness level `2`, mirroring smooth matte kitchenware, rounded table corners, and organic produce shapes.

### Corner Radius Standards
- **Base (0.5rem / 8px):** Input fields, ingredient strike-through rows, small interactive buttons, and table rows.
- **Large (1rem / 16px):** Standard recipe cards, floating timer containers, cookbook cover preview tiles, and step panels.
- **Extra Large (1.5rem / 24px):** Primary bottom sheets, cook-mode full-screen panels, and hero media overlays.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Dietary badges, cooking duration pills, unit switches (Metric/Imperial), and primary floating action buttons (FABs).

## Components

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary Buttons:** Solid Basil Green (`#2D6A4F`) background with Crisp White text. Radius is rounded (`0.5rem`) for standard buttons, and pill for action-oriented triggers. Hover shifts to `#1B4332`.
- **Timer / Alert Buttons:** Warm Saffron (`#E76F51`) background for active running timers or primary Cook Mode actions.
- **Secondary Buttons:** Pale Basil Mist (`#F4F7F5`) fill with `#2D6A4F` text and an interior soft border.

### Recipe Cards & Grids
- **Card Structure:** 16:9 or 4:3 high-res photography header with bottom container padded by `space-md`.
- **Overlay Pills:** Floating badges on image top corners for cook time (e.g., "25 min" with a miniature clock icon) and difficulty ("Easy", "Intermediate").
- **Dietary Badges:** Micro-pills rendered in subtle tinted backgrounds (e.g., Pale Sage for "Gluten-Free", Pale Citrus for "Vegan").

### Ingredient Lists & Unit Controls
- **Unit Toggle:** Compact segmented pill control at the head of the ingredient section switching between **Metric** and **Imperial** without reloading.
- **Interactive Checklist:** Large touch target rows (minimum 44px height). Checking an item applies a soft strikethrough, shifts text to Muted Slate (`#64748B`), and changes the checkbox icon from an empty slate ring to a solid Basil Green check.

### Step-by-Step Instruction Blocks
- **Numbered Steps:** Large Outfit numeral in a muted circular container (`#F4F7F5`), turning Saffron (`#E76F51`) when that step is active.
- **Inline Smart Timers:** Time references within text (e.g., "Simmer for 12 minutes") automatically render as interactive pill chips. Clicking initiates a dockable countdown with audio-visual cues.

### Cookbook Binders & Collections
- **Spine & Cover Personalization:** Digital binder mockups featuring custom spine color swatches (Basil Green, Citrus Zest, Berry Plum, Olive Slate).
- **Cookbook Card:** Displays custom title in Outfit bold, total recipe count, and miniature photo previews in a modular 3-tile mosaic.

### Kitchen & Cook Mode HUD
- **Entry Point:** A persistent floating action pill: "Start Cook Mode" with a flame or chef-knife glyph.
- **Interface Behavior:** High-contrast full-screen view with enlarged step typography (`headline-lg`), high-visibility ingredient callouts per step, screen wake-lock enabled by default, and gesture-friendly previous/next targets.
